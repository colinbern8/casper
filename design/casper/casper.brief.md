# Casper wordmark: final art brief

Subject:     "Casper" wordmark only (no separate symbol or ghost)
Style:       Fraunces Black (soft 100, wonky 1), tracking -2, outlined paths
Palette:     neutral only. Light mode: #171717 on #fafafa. Dark mode: #fafafa on #0a0a0a
Background:  transparent
Output:      SVG, viewBox ~392x138

Files
- casper-wordmark-light.svg  dark text, for light backgrounds
- casper-wordmark-dark.svg   light text, for dark backgrounds
- casper-wordmark.svg        currentColor, follows the surrounding text color
- casper-wordmark-themed.svg switches itself with prefers-color-scheme (use as a standalone image)

Provenance: outlined from Fraunces (Google Fonts, OFL) with fontTools; Recraft MCP was unavailable, so this is the hand-authored fallback.
History: ghost marks and the other five font options were rejected and removed.
